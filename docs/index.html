Paper Code Docs

Data-use auditing detects whether data was used in training; watermarking dominates. Providers may still evade after training on unauthorized copyrighted text.

Data-level evasion degrades corpora; model-level evasion suppresses audit signals at inference via a lightweight SBE. Logit Laundering weakens STAMP, Radioactive, Top-1, and related auditors while preserving knowledge and generation quality.

  • Motivation. Data-level attacks harm corpus quality and leave residual signals—motivating model-level evasion.
  • Method. Inference-time logit reshaping suppresses watermark statistics without retraining or corrupting corpora (Method).
  • Findings. Five models, multiple auditors: p-values rise to non-significance with knowledge and quality largely retained (Results).

Method

Data-level attacks degrade text quality; model-level baselines like Rank-Swap still leave STAMP/Radioactive-visible signals. Logit Laundering reshapes logits at inference to suppress watermark-induced bias.

At each decoding step, logits are reshaped as

ztBob = ztbase − λt · ztSBE

Pipeline:

  1. Continual pretraining. Base model on public Dpub + watermarked copyrighted Dwm.
  2. Train SBE. Fit watermark bias on Dwm (ℒfit); flatten unrelated bias on Dpub (ℒflat).
  3. Inference. Subtract scaled SBE logits; λt from top-1/top-2 margin under a JS budget.

Higher-confidence steps allow stronger suppression; JS budget caps drift from the base distribution.

The steps above summarize the paper pipeline. For the full reproduction workflow (watermark → train-base → SBE training → calibrate → auditing & evaluation), see the README.
